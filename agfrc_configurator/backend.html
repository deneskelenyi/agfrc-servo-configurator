Parameter guide — what each setting does
Travel Range R / L
Endpoint adjustment — how far the horn travels each way from neutral. The two sides are independent. Reduce a side if the horn or linkage binds at that end of travel.
Damping Factor
How strongly the servo brakes as it reaches the target. Higher values stop overshoot and buzzing/hunting; too low and it bounces past the target, too high and it feels lazy.
Sensitivity
How small a change in the input signal the servo reacts to (deadband). Higher = responds to finer stick movements. The original software notes values of 10 or below also enable extra protection.
PWM Power
Maximum motor drive power, as a percentage. 100% gives full torque and speed; lowering it reduces current draw, heat and holding power.
Soft Start Level
On power-up the servo ramps gently to its position instead of snapping to it. Higher level = slower, softer start. Useful when a model powers up with a control surface deflected.
Overload Protection Levels 1–3
Three staged protections against stalls. If the servo is overloaded continuously for a level's time, motor power is cut to that level's duty percentage. Level 1 triggers first, level 3 last. Protects the motor and electronics from burnout during a jam.
Lose PPM Protection
Failsafe when the control signal is lost: Keep Position holds the last commanded position; Go Neutral Position returns the horn to center.
Inversion
Reverses the servo's direction of rotation — use it instead of reversing the channel in the radio.
